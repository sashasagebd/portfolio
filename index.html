About Me

I'm a computer science student at the University of Idaho (B.S. expected May 2027) who likes writing code outside of class as much as inside it — usually a React or TypeScript project that started as "let me try something" and turned into a real app. My coursework has taken me through data structures, algorithms, databases, computer systems, machine learning, and compilers.

I'm currently a web developer intern at MedConnect, helping with a full redesign of the non-profit's website. Outside of that I build full projects front to back: React/TypeScript on the frontend, Express or Go on the backend depending on what a project needs. I also run a self-hosted homelab, and I like the part of development where a feature that looked simple turns out to have an interesting bug underneath it — and fixing that bug even more.

Besides schoolwork, I also enjoy all sorts of outdoor activities, hanging out with friends, and getting lost in a good book, movie, or video game.
